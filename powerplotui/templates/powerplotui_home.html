Powerplot is used to chart and review the statistics derived from the Powermap and Powermatch processes, and to and actual (SCADA) performance of the South West Interconnected System (SWIS). Working left to right across the menu:

  1. Variants Statistics – show plots of Baseline Variant sensitivity analysese, or export to Excel.
  2. Renewable Energy Target – the RET Dashboard (monthly, quarterly and annual reports), Scenario Projections to 2040, Manage Targets, and the RET Replacement Scenarios analysis and its configuration.
  3. Facility Generation – plot actual (SCADA) and simulated (SAM) output for facilities and technologies, analyse SCADA load by period, and compare actual with simulated generation.
  4. Risk Analysis – record risk events against a Facilities scenario, review the 6x6 risk matrix and compare the risk profile of scenarios.
  5. Transport Electrification – track EV uptake against the CSIRO projections, and view the AEMO EV charging profiles that drive the EV load model.
  6. WEM Prices – 3D history of wholesale energy prices in the Wholesale Electricity Market (WEM).
  7. SWIS Demand – 3D history of operational and underlying demand.
  8. ESOO Bias Tracking – check the AEMO ESOO demand forecasts against what actually happened, to see whether they have been systematically high or low.
  9. Powerplot Help – detailed instructions for every page (use Powerplot Help Edit to maintain it).

Most pages draw on data prepared earlier in the workflow: Variants Statistics needs a Baseline and Variants run in Powermatch; the Simulated (SAM) plots need Run Power to have been run for the scenario; and the Actual (SCADA) pages and trackers depend on the data feeds maintained in Powermap (Data Take-up menu).
