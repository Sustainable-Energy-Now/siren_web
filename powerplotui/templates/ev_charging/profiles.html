EV Charging Profiles

The daily charging shapes (from AEMO's IASR EV workbook, Step Change scenario) that turn a projected annual EV energy figure into a half-hourly load trace.

AEMO models EV charging as a mix of charging types — behaviours like "plug in on arrival" or "charge overnight on a time-of-use tariff". Each type has a characteristic time-of-day shape (what fraction of that vehicle's daily charging energy lands in each half-hour) and a share (what fraction of the fleet charges that way). The EV load model spreads the CSIRO-projected annual energy across the year using the share-weighted combination of these shapes — so the shape of WA's future EV load peak depends entirely on this page's data, not on the uptake projection.

How these profiles are used

  1. Each charging type's weekday and weekend shape is normalised to sum to 1 across the 48 half-hours.
  2. Shapes are combined into one composite weekday shape and one composite weekend shape per charging mode, weighted by each type's share of charging (combine_charging_type_shapes).
  3. The composite is tiled across every real calendar day of the target year (weekday vs weekend by the actual calendar) and scaled so its integral equals the projected annual EV energy to within 0.01% (shape_annual_energy_to_halfhourly).
  4. The unmanaged composite is the baseline. The managed composite is used where AEMO publishes real TOU / off-peak shapes for the region; otherwise a clearly-labelled synthetic redistribution lever is applied instead.

Charging modes

unmanaged
Arrival-based charging (e.g. "Unscheduled", "Public"). Peaks in the afternoon/evening as people get home or park — the worst case for the system peak. This is the baseline the model reports.
managed
Time-of-use, off-peak or coordinated charging (e.g. "Off-peak and Solar", "TOU Grid Solar"). Shifts load to the middle of the day (solar) or overnight.
v2x
Vehicle-to-home / vehicle-to-grid. Recorded for completeness but excluded from trace synthesis this Sprint (dormant, FR-17).
other
A source label the classifier didn't recognise — excluded until classified, never silently mis-bucketed.

Caveats

  • Step Change scenario, single vintage. Shares are AEMO's Step Change behaviour; only the CSIRO uptake varies in the model, not these shapes.
  • Unweighted vehicle-class mean. Each row is averaged across AEMO's finer vehicle-class breakdown by simple mean, not fleet-weighted — so raw shares need not sum to exactly 100% (the composite re-normalises internally, so trace energy is still exact).
  • No static shape for dynamic charging. AEMO's "TOU Dynamic Charging" has no fixed time-of-day profile by design, so its share is absent from the managed composite — treat the managed composite as a lower bound on real managed-charging uptake.
  • WEM region. The 2025 workbook publishes a WA-specific ('WEM') profile directly; older vintages would require borrowing a NEM region (obstacle O5).
  • Managed lever is illustrative. Where a synthetic redistribution kernel is used instead of real TOU shapes, it fixes only the objective family ("minimise SWIS net load"), not an FR-18-approved constraint set.
