Powermatch is used for matching and balancing Renewable Energy resources to the load on the South West Interconnected System (SWIS). It takes the Facilities scenarios built in Powermap together with a demand forecast, then quantifies and costs the dispatchable energy generation, storage and CO2-e emissions. The parameters passed to Powermatch are stored in database tables. Working left to right across the menu:

  1. Demand Forecasting – build or select the demand forecast (load trace) for the target year, from ESOO data, EV load, or factor-based projection.
  2. Run Power – use the System Advisor Model (SAM) developed by the National Renewable Energy Laboratory (NREL) and weather data to generate the hourly output of the wind and solar facilities in a scenario for a reference year's weather.
  3. Set Merit Order – set the dispatch order of the technologies, plus the carbon price and discount rate.
  4. Baseline Scenario – choose a Facilities scenario and demand forecast, adjust technology capacities, and run Powermatch to establish the baseline.
  5. Create Variants – perform sensitivity analysis on a technology's attributes across a numberruns to optimise outcomes such as emissions or levelised cost of energy (LCOE).

Facilities scenarios and the data feeds they depend on are maintained in Powermap (see its Data Take-up menu).

Once a Baseline and Variations have been created, continue in Powerplot, where Variants Statistics can be used to plot the outcomes.
