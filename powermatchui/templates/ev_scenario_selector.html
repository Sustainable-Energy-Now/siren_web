EV Charging Load Scenario (Outcome A: Sensitivity)

Pick a half-hourly base scenario and an EV uptake scenario (Low/Medium/High), and build a derived Powermatch scenario whose demand is the base trace plus the selected EV charging load, added as a toggleable layer (FR-11). EV energy is AEMO's 2025 IASR WEM trajectory (CSIRO 2025 projections developed for AEMO): Low / Medium / High are Slower Growth / Step Change / Accelerated Transition. The base scenario itself is never modified — removing the EV layer just means using the base scenario directly. To see Low/Medium/High side by side without building anything, use the sensitivity comparison (FR-12).
