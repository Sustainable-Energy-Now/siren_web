Facilities Scenarios

Create or update Facilities Take-up scenarios for future years. Each Low / Expected / High scenario is a facility portfolio built from every facility's status, commissioning date, retirement date and commissioning probability. Re-running for the same year and band refreshes the existing scenario.

  • Low – commissioned and under-construction facilities only.
  • Expected – plus proposed/planned facilities whose effective commissioning probability meets the threshold below.
  • High – plus all proposed/planned facilities due by the year.

Facilities retired by the year are excluded from every band; proposed/planned facilities with no commissioning date are skipped.
