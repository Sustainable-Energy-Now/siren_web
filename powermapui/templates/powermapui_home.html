Powermap is the Siren module that describes the generation and transmission infrastructure of the South West Interconnected System (SWIS). Facilities are grid infrastructure that use a particular technology, and a Scenario is a collection of Facilities of different technologies. Powermap's output — Facilities scenarios and the grid they connect to — is the input to the Powermatch module. Working left to right across the menu:

  1. Grid Map – view, create and edit facilities, grid lines and terminals geographically, and check transmission losses.
  2. Scenarios – review the Scenarios matrix and build or select the Facilities scenarios (the facility/technology portfolios) to be modelled.
  3. Technologies – maintain the technology definitions behind the facilities, including wind turbine and storage types.
  4. Facilities – maintain the individual wind, solar, storage and generator facilities, and review the project pipeline: network view, timeline, capacity outlook and build probability.
  5. Transmission – maintain terminals (substations) and grid lines, run health checks, and track the Clean Energy Link (CEL) expansion.
  6. SWIS Boundary – edit the SWIS boundary polygon and apportion postcodes to it. Used to identify EV registrations within the SWIS.
  7. Data Take-up – monitor and keep the external data feeds (ESOO, EV, SCADA, CSIRO GenCost) up to date through the Data Pipelines dashboard and GenCost Data pages.
  8. Table Update – make bulk edits to reference tables. Deprecated.

Once a Facilities scenario is ready, continue in Powermatch, where Run Power uses the System Advisor Model (SAM) developed by the National Renewable Energy Laboratory (NREL) and weather data to generate each facility's output.
